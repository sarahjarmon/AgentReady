    Frequently Asked Questions

    What exactly does AgentReady analyze?

    AgentReady analyzes the public signals available on your website and evaluates three dimensions: Visibility, Understanding and Buyability. It shows what could be verified, where evidence is limited, and what you should prioritize next.

    What do Visibility, Understanding and Buyability mean?

    Visibility measures whether AI agents can discover useful information about your business. Understanding measures whether they can clearly interpret what you offer. Buyability measures whether they can identify and navigate toward the next commercial action, such as buying, booking or contacting you.

    Do I need technical skills?

    No. AgentReady is designed to turn technical AI-readiness signals into clear evidence, scores and prioritized actions that business owners, marketers and small teams can understand.

    Why does AgentReady sometimes show a dash instead of a score?

    AgentReady does not invent certainty when there is not enough observable evidence. If a dimension cannot be assessed reliably from the public signals available, it shows that limitation instead of manufacturing a misleading score.

    Am I locked into a contract?

    No. The Founder Plan is a month-to-month subscription. You can cancel anytime.

    What happens to my Founder price if I cancel?

    Your $39/month Founder price remains locked while your subscription stays continuously active. If you cancel and later return, the Founder price may no longer be available and the current standard price will apply.
